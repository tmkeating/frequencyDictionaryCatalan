Automates the process of grabbing example sentences and converting them into cloze-deletion anki cards. Uses the dataset from the study titled "Prevalence norms for 40,777 Catalan words: An online megastudy of vocabulary size".
It's a list of 40,777 Catalan words ordered from most common to least.

Link to the datset: https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Supplementary_materials_for_Prevalence_norms_for_40_777_Catalan_words_An_online_megastudy_of_vocabulary_size_/16622536/3?file=36145509
